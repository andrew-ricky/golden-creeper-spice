About Golden Creeper Spice Garden

Golden Creeper Spice & Herbal Garden is a tourist-based Ayurvedic company founded more than 3 decades ago in Ahungalla, Sri Lanka. We pride ourselves on being the best, offering top-quality service and authentic organic products to our clientele.

Clients are treated to our warm local hospitality and shown around the garden with a walk led by our expert local guides, who share deep knowledge of traditional Ayurvedic lore.

Customers are welcomed with a refreshing hot spice tea and shown the medicinal benefits derived from a vast array of herbs and trees.

Guests can also witness live usage demonstrations of natural medicines and purchase attractively displayed products directly from our garden store.
